# SOP-HAZ-002: Hazardous Material Transport and Zone Isolation

## 1. Scope
Applies to handling chemical reagents, volatile liquids, and flammable inventory within designated warehouse containment zones (Sector H).

## 2. Speed Limits for Hazmat Payloads
- AMRs carrying flagged hazardous inventory (`HAZMAT=True`) must not exceed 0.8 m/s under any circumstances.
- Regular transit corridors are strictly off-limits; transport routes must utilize containment perimeter lanes.

## 3. Containment Zone Access Rules
- Only AMRs equipped with certified sealed chassis (Chassis Type C or above) may enter Sector H.
- No concurrent transport of incompatible chemicals is permitted within a 3-meter radius.
