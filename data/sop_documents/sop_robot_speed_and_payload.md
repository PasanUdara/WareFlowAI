# SOP-SPD-003: AMR Speed Limits and Payload Thresholds

## 1. Speed Guidelines
- **Main Aisles (Clear Line of Sight)**: Maximum speed 2.0 m/s.
- **Narrow Aisles (Storage Racks)**: Maximum speed 1.2 m/s.
- **Packing & Workstation Areas**: Maximum speed 0.6 m/s.

## 2. Payload Capacities
- Standard AMR Payload Cap: 50.0 kg.
- Heavy-Duty AMR Payload Cap: 100.0 kg.
- Orders exceeding 100.0 kg must be decomposed into multiple subtasks or assigned across multiple AMR dispatches.
